## Booking changes, late arrival and no-shows
A booked table is kept for 15 minutes after the booked time. After that it may be given to other guests.
You can change or cancel a booking free of charge by calling 07-765-4321 until 2 hours before the booked time.
The chat can make new bookings, but it cannot change or cancel them: please call the café.
If a booking for 5 or more people does not arrive and does not call, that phone number can only book by phone for the next 30 days.
We do not take a deposit for table bookings.

## The study room (back room)
The study room at the back seats up to 10 people and has a whiteboard, a TV screen with HDMI, and power outlets at every seat.
It can be booked only by phone, at least 1 day in advance, in blocks of 2 hours.
The room fee is NT$300 for 2 hours, and every person in the room still orders at least one item (the normal minimum order).
Students with a valid NKUST student card pay NT$200 for the room on weekdays.
Please keep your voice down when the door is open, and leave the room clean when you go.

## Allergens and special diets
Our kitchen uses milk, eggs, wheat (gluten), nuts, soy and sesame every day.
The Pesto Pasta contains pine nuts and cheese. The Basque Cheesecake and the Tiramisu contain milk and eggs, and the Tiramisu contains alcohol (coffee liqueur).
The Scone and the Butter Croissant contain wheat, milk and butter.
Oat milk is our only plant milk; we do not have soy milk or almond milk. Oat milk is made in a factory that also handles gluten.
We cannot promise that any item is completely free of an allergen. Guests with a serious allergy should tell the staff before ordering.
We have no fully vegan meal. The Lemon Soda, the Earl Grey Tea, the Americano and the Cold Brew contain no animal products.

## Caffeine, decaf and children
We do not serve decaf coffee at the moment.
For guests who want no caffeine, we suggest the Lemon Soda or the Hot Chocolate. The Earl Grey Tea and the Matcha Latte contain caffeine.
A Regular hand-drip or Cold Brew has more caffeine than an Americano. Pregnant guests may ask the staff for advice.
Children are welcome. We have two high chairs; please ask at the counter.

## Wrong drinks, refunds and complaints
If your drink or food is wrong, or not hot or cold as ordered, tell the staff within 15 minutes and we will make it again free of charge.
We do not give money back for items that have already been eaten or drunk more than half.
If you paid by card, LINE Pay or JKOPAY and the amount was wrong, the money goes back the same way within 7 working days.
Complaints can be made to the staff, by phone, or by email to hello@harborbean.example within 7 days of the visit. We answer within 3 working days.

## Lost and found
Items left in the café are kept for 14 days.
Phones, wallets, keys and student cards are kept in the office and need photo ID, or a description of the item, to collect.
Umbrellas, cups and clothes are kept in the box by the door.
Food, drinks and opened packages are thrown away at closing time.
After 14 days, items that nobody collects are given to the NKUST lost-and-found office or a charity.

## Outside food, drinks and birthday cakes
Outside food and drinks are not allowed, except baby food and water.
You may bring your own birthday cake. The cake fee is NT$50 per cake, and we lend plates, forks and a knife.
Candles are fine; sparklers and party poppers are not allowed indoors.

## Laptops, phone calls and noise
Laptops are welcome. The long bar table and the window seats have power outlets.
On Saturday and Sunday the 2-hour table limit also applies to laptop users. When the café is full, staff may ask guests who have finished to share a table.
Please take long phone calls and video meetings outside, or use headphones and keep your voice low.
Do not move tables or chairs between the window seats and the main room.

## Takeaway pre-orders by phone
You can order takeaway by phone (07-765-4321) during staff phone hours. Drinks are ready in about 10 minutes, food in about 20 minutes.
A pre-order is kept at the counter for 30 minutes after the ready time. Drinks made more than 30 minutes ago are not made again for free.
Pre-orders of more than 10 drinks need 1 hour's notice.
Takeaway cups and bags are free. Bring your own cup to get the own-cup discount, also for takeaway.

## Coffee beans: grinding, freshness and returns
We grind beans for free when you buy them: tell the staff your brewing method (hand-drip, espresso, French press, moka pot).
All beans are roasted in our shop every Wednesday, and the roast date is on the bag. Beans are best within 30 days of roasting.
Keep beans in the closed bag, away from sunlight, and not in the fridge.
Unopened bags can be returned within 7 days with the receipt. Opened or ground beans cannot be returned.

## Gift vouchers
We sell paper gift vouchers of NT$300 and NT$500 at the counter.
A gift voucher is valid for 12 months from the date printed on it. It cannot be exchanged for cash, and no change is given.
A lost voucher cannot be replaced. Vouchers can be used together with the member discount and the own-cup discount.

## Typhoons and special closing days
When the Kaohsiung City Government announces that work and classes are cancelled because of a typhoon, the café is closed for that day.
Bookings on that day are cancelled automatically, with no penalty.
We also close for the Lunar New Year eve and the first two days of the Lunar New Year. Other closing days are posted on our Instagram one week before.
